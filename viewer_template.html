What is known

KPower lists V2 walls as 2.54 mm and the flange as 9.525 mm. The version in the reference photos is not confirmed.

ACKOJA's OEM-pattern A26-0159 replacement is listed as 456 x 270 x 140 mm, separately from packaging dimensions. Its overall length and width constrain this study's provisional scale. These are not verified mounting-plate dimensions or proof of K24Z3 compatibility.

What is estimated

Revised rear sump: the bung-side wing has been pulled inward 30 mm in flange coordinates, bringing its shoulder into balance with the opposite side. The narrow throat and broad straight rear edge remain. The bung, bore and plug follow the inset wall. These are photo-proportion estimates. Bottom shows engine front at the top and rear at the bottom for comparison.

Revised flange: asymmetric perimeter, inner opening and all 16 hole centres are traced from your gasket image, including its unequal end chamfers and rounded corners. The photo supplies relative geometry, not an absolute scale or confirmed handedness.

ATI documents an OEM K20/K24 slant of 15°. KPower's K24Z3 guide does not give a numerical installation angle, so this is an editable assumption, not a confirmed KPower specification. The walls and baffles follow the flange axes; the sump and bridge floors are cut level. The lean direction has been reversed per your correction. Individual wall orientations remain approximate.

Overall length/width, depths, absolute bolt coordinates, drain bores and hidden baffles remain estimates. The model captures the two deep wells and shallow rack/subframe clearance bridge. It omits the separate pump adapter and pickup tube, weld texture, threads and exact trap-door hinges.

Scale: CAD and STL coordinates are millimetres. GLB uses metres. Z = 0 is the small-well bottom; the flange center is above it. Depth parameters are vertical distances below the flange center, not flange-normal depths. The small box well is at negative X; the broad polygonal well is at positive X. Positive Y is the high side for positive roll. Vehicle orientation is inferred.
